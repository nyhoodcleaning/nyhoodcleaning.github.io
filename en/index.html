What affects your hood cleaning quote?

A hood-only job and a system that includes long ducts and a rooftop fan involve different work. A useful quote starts with clear equipment details.

  1. Hood length and number of filters
  2. Grease buildup and time since the last cleaning
  3. Duct length, bends and accessible cleaning openings
  4. Fan type and roof access
  5. Additional equipment, including an ESP unit
  6. Restaurant location and preferred service time

NYC hood cleaning: common questions

What does restaurant hood cleaning include?

The agreed scope may include the hood, filters, accessible grease ducts and exhaust fan. These items are not automatically included in every package. We review the equipment and access with you before confirming the work.

How much does hood cleaning cost in NYC?

Pricing depends on hood length, filter count, grease condition, duct access, rooftop equipment and the service scope. Send photos and your restaurant address for an initial quote; some systems require an on-site assessment.

How often should a restaurant schedule cleaning?

Cooking volume, operating hours, equipment and grease buildup affect the service schedule. Follow applicable local requirements and arrange an assessment if you notice heavy grease, dripping or a change in exhaust performance.

Can you clean the rooftop exhaust fan too?

Yes, rooftop fan cleaning is among our services. Let us know the fan type, roof access arrangements and whether you want fan cleaning included in the quote.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Queens, Brooklyn, Manhattan, the Bronx and Staten Island, including Flushing and Chinatown. We also handle inquiries for Long Island, New Jersey, Connecticut and Pennsylvania; scheduling depends on the location and scope.
