Licensed Precipitator Cleaning in Manhattan

If your restaurant has an electrostatic precipitator—also called an ESP or electronic air cleaner—include it in your cleaning request. Cleaning the kitchen hood alone does not mean the precipitator has also been serviced.

Best Commercial Hood Cleaning holds a precipitator-cleaning license. We provide cleaning for applicable ESP components based on the unit, its condition and the access available. You can request our license details when discussing the job.

What to Send for an ESP Quote

  • A photo of the entire unit and its location.
  • A clear photo of the brand and model label.
  • The number of units and cells, if known.
  • The last cleaning date, if available.
  • Any warning lights or performance concerns.
  • Access restrictions and available service times.

What We Discuss Before Service

  • Which components can be accessed and cleaned.
  • The condition of removable filters and cells.
  • Equipment shutdown and safe access arrangements.
  • Cleaning, drying and reassembly requirements.
  • Whether hood and filter cleaning is also requested.
  • Any issues that may need separate repair service.

Cleaning and Electrical Repair Are Different Services

An alarm or loss of performance does not always mean the unit simply needs cleaning. Electrical faults, damaged components and control problems may require a qualified repair technician. ESP equipment contains high-voltage components; do not open an energized unit to take photos.

Manhattan Hood & ESP Cleaning FAQs

Do you provide precipitator cleaning in Manhattan?

Yes. We hold a precipitator-cleaning license and offer ESP cleaning in Manhattan. Send the equipment brand, model, photos and location so we can discuss the applicable scope.

Is precipitator cleaning included in a hood cleaning quote?

It should be specifically listed if requested. Hood cleaning and ESP cleaning involve different components. Tell us you have a precipitator so it can be assessed and included in the proposal where applicable.

Can you remove thick, stubborn grease?

We use specialized grease-scraping tools on suitable, accessible surfaces to help remove thick deposits. The method depends on the surface and equipment; delicate ESP components are handled differently.

Do you clean Chinatown restaurant kitchens?

Yes. Contact us about hood and precipitator cleaning for Chinatown restaurants and other Manhattan commercial kitchens. English and Chinese inquiries are welcome.

Can cleaning be scheduled outside our cooking hours?

Send your operating hours and preferred service window. We will discuss availability and the time needed for access, equipment shutdown and cleaning before confirming a booking.

How much does Manhattan hood cleaning cost?

The quote depends on hood size, filter quantity, grease condition, accessible equipment and the requested scope. ESP units and additional equipment should be identified separately so the proposal reflects your kitchen.

Will cleaning fix an ESP alarm?

Not necessarily. Contamination can be one possible issue, but alarms may also involve damaged parts or electrical faults. Tell us about the symptoms when requesting service; separate diagnosis or repair may be needed.

How often should our equipment be cleaned?

The schedule should consider applicable requirements, manufacturer instructions, cooking volume and actual grease buildup. Share your equipment details and cleaning history when discussing a maintenance plan.
