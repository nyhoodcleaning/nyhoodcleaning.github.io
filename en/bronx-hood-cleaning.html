What Helps Us Give You a Clear Quote?

Two kitchens with similar hood lengths can require different amounts of work. Filter quantity, grease buildup, system access and additional equipment all affect the proposed service.

Send These Details

  • Restaurant name and Bronx street address.
  • Photos showing each hood and its filters.
  • Approximate hood length and filter quantity.
  • Last professional cleaning date, if known.
  • Any heavy grease or access concerns.
  • Your preferred cleaning day and time.

Identify Additional Equipment

  • Any separate exhaust fans to be cleaned.
  • ESP or precipitator equipment, if installed.
  • Multiple hoods or separate cooking lines.
  • Available duct access openings.
  • Locked equipment rooms or access doors.
  • Building-management coordination requirements.

Bronx Hood Cleaning FAQs

Which parts of the Bronx do you serve?

We offer service throughout the Bronx, including Fordham, Belmont, Mott Haven, Riverdale, Pelham Bay, Throgs Neck and surrounding neighborhoods. Send your address to discuss availability.

Do you clean hoods in takeout restaurants and delis?

Yes. We provide hood cleaning for takeout restaurants, delis, pizzerias and other commercial kitchens. Send photos of your hood and cooking line so we can discuss the appropriate scope.

Can you remove thick grease buildup?

We use specialized grease-scraping tools on suitable, accessible surfaces to help remove heavy deposits. The cleaning approach depends on the equipment, material and condition.

Does the quote include every part of the exhaust system?

The quote should identify the included components and accessible areas. Filters, duct sections, fans and ESP units should be discussed explicitly so you know what is included before booking.

Do you offer precipitator cleaning?

Yes. We hold a precipitator-cleaning license. Send the unit’s brand, model, exterior photos and location to discuss the cleaning scope. Electrical diagnosis or repair may require a separate technician.

Can you work around our restaurant’s operating hours?

Tell us your opening hours and preferred service window. We will discuss availability, access and the time needed for equipment shutdown before confirming the appointment.

How much does Bronx hood cleaning cost?

Pricing depends on hood size, filter quantity, grease condition, equipment access and the requested work. Send photos and your restaurant address for a quote based on your kitchen.

Can I request a quote in Chinese?
