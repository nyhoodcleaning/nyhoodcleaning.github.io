What affects your Queens hood cleaning quote?

Hood length is a starting point, but it does not describe the whole job. A clear quote accounts for the requested equipment and its condition.

  • Hood size: Approximate length and number of hood sections.
  • Filter quantity: How many filters are installed and whether cleaning is requested.
  • Grease condition: Visible buildup and time since the last professional cleaning.
  • Access: Available openings, equipment layout and building arrangements.
  • Additional work: Any other components or tasks you want included.
  • Scheduling: Operating hours and your preferred service window.
