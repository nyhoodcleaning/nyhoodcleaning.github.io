Why It Matters: NYC Restaurant Hood Cleaning Service: Compliance Isn’t Optional

NFPA 96 and NYC fire code

Under NFPA 96 and NYC fire code, commercial kitchens must maintain a scheduled exhaust cleaning program based on cooking volume. Skipping it means FDNY violations, fines starting at $1,000, and risk of forced closure. We connect NYC restaurant operators with certified hood cleaning professionals who handle the entire process — and leave you with the documentation FDNY inspectors look for.

Every Service, Cleaned to Bare Metal

01 Hood Canopy Degreasing Full interior and exterior degreasing of the hood canopy, stripped to bare metal — the surface FDNY inspectors check first.

02 Grease Filter Service Every baffle and mesh filter removed, degreased, and reinstalled — the first line of defense against grease fires.

03 Ductwork Cleaning Accessible and inaccessible duct sections treated and documented — the part of the system most often skipped by uncertified cleaners.

04 Exhaust Fan & Containment Fan blades, housing, and motor area degreased. Grease containment trays emptied, cleaned, and repositioned.
