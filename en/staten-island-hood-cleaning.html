Accessible Duct Cleaning

What to Confirm Before Cleaning Day

01 · SCOPE

Which Equipment Is Included?

Review the hoods, filters and other exhaust components listed in the proposal. Discuss any additional work before the appointment.

02 · TIMING

When Can Cooking Stop?

Coordinate the service window with kitchen staff and discuss the shutdown and preparation needed for the equipment being cleaned.

03 · ACCESS

Who Will Open the Required Areas?

Make sure the on-site contact can provide access to the kitchen and any agreed equipment locations.

Booking for More Than One Location?

Send a separate address, equipment list and photo set for each restaurant. This helps avoid assuming that the same cleaning scope applies to every kitchen.

A Clean Exterior Is Only Part of the Picture

Wiping visible stainless steel does not reveal what is behind the filters. When arranging professional service, discuss the internal areas to be cleaned and any sections that cannot be reached through existing access points.

If you notice thick deposits or are unsure when the system was last cleaned, describe the condition when contacting us. Photos can help start the discussion, although some details may need an on-site assessment.
